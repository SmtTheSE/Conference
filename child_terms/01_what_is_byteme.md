# What Is ByteMe? (Simple Version)
### If someone asks "so what does your project actually do?" — say this.

---

## The One-Sentence Answer

ByteMe is like a **smart price-checker for houses** in Southeast Asia — it tells you if a property is priced fairly, too expensive, or secretly a great deal, using data instead of a salesperson's word.

---

## The Problem We Solved (The Story)

Imagine you want to buy a house in the Philippines.

You go to an agent. They say: "This condo costs ₱9,000,000 — it's a great deal!"

But how do you know if that's actually true?

You don't. The agent gets paid when you buy, so they'll always say it's a great deal. There's no website like Zillow in the Philippines. There's no central database of real prices. You're just trusting a stranger with millions of pesos.

**That's the problem. Information asymmetry.** (Big word — it just means: the seller knows way more than the buyer. The playing field is unfair.)

ByteMe fixes that. It's a computer system that looks at real data — thousands of past sales, rental records, news, community reports — and tells you the honest price.

---

## How It Works (The Simple Version)

Think of it like this:

> ByteMe is a student who studied 57,000+ house prices from 5 countries, then you show it a new house and ask "is this a fair price?" — and it tells you YES or NO with numbers to back it up.

There are 4 tools inside ByteMe:

### Tool 1 — The Price Checker (PH Valuator)
You type in: city, size, bedrooms, type of property.
It tells you: the fair price range, monthly rent estimate, and whether the listing is overpriced or undervalued.

Example answer it gives:
> "This 60sqm condo in Iloilo should cost ₱8.5M–₱9.9M. The listing price is ₱12.5M. That's 31% overpriced. Warning: speculative premium detected."

### Tool 2 — The Opportunity Finder (Scanner)
It scans all the zones we studied and finds the ones that are UNDERVALUED right now — places where prices haven't caught up to how popular the area is becoming.

Like finding a stock before it goes up.

### Tool 3 — The Legal Helper (Cultural AI)
It answers questions like:
- "Can a foreigner buy land here?"
- "What are the flood risks in this area?"
- "What laws apply to this investment?"

It knows Philippine property laws and ASEAN investment rules.

### Tool 4 — The International Comparer (Global Intel)
It compares the same type of property across countries — Philippines vs Thailand vs Vietnam vs Malaysia — so you can see where your money goes furthest.

---

## Why It Matters

**For regular people:** First-time buyers and Overseas Filipino Workers (OFWs) sending money home to invest can now get an independent, data-backed answer instead of trusting an agent.

**For the region:** When buyers have real data, sellers can't inflate prices unfairly. Markets become honest.

**The big idea:** We're giving regular people access to the same quality of information that only big investment firms had before.
