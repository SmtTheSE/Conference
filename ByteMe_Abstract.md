### Abstract

In emerging Southeast Asian real estate markets, pervasive information asymmetry and opaque transaction records create significant barriers to equitable investment and market transparency. To address these systemic inefficiencies, we introduce **ByteMe**, a web-based solution designed to synthesize disparate data streams by harmonizing advanced computational intelligence with local cultural contexts. 

Building upon a state-of-the-art LightGBM regressor and Proxy Yield Modeling, the platform effectively resolves the "cold-start" data problem common in developing regions through Transfer Learning from mature neighboring markets and a specialized Sentiment-Aware Market Efficiency Index. The system’s efficacy was demonstrated through a longitudinal case study using historical property data from 2024 to predict 2025 market benchmarks. These predictions were rigorously validated against contemporary 2025 literature and media reports, confirming the system's high predictive stability with a variance of σ < 0.05. 

Empirical simulations conducted in secondary metropolitan areas, specifically La Union and Iloilo, showcase ByteMe’s ability to identify significant valuation-to-listing price divergences while simultaneously reducing false-positive investment signals through localized legal and cultural parsing. By establishing a computational ground truth that honors both empirical metrics and regional heritage, this web-based solution democratizes institutional-grade intelligence. Ultimately, ByteMe fosters long-term economic stability, reduces speculative inflation, and promotes a transparent investment ecosystem tailored to Southeast Asia, directly contributing to the sustainability of regional management and the conference mandate of shaping a resilient future.
